Poland Helper Privacy Policy

Updated 4 October 2026. The controller of personal data submitted in reports about Poland Helper is Hleb Leonchyk. For privacy enquiries, contact hello@callandcraft.com. The app is an independent guide for foreign nationals in Poland, not a government service.

Data we handle. Reading guides and downloading original blank PDF forms does not require an account or personal details. The selected language and search terms are used to display content within the app; we do not send them to our own server. If you save a report draft, its title and text are stored locally in the app and are not sent automatically. A PDF you save stays in the folder you choose on your device.

Error reports. The report button opens a Google Form in the selected language in your browser. When opened from a guide, only its public title is pre-filled in the form URL. The form asks for the guide or screen name and a description of the error; Google also records the response time. Submission occurs only when you press Submit on Google's page. Do not enter a PESEL, address, medical information, document scans or another person's data. Email collection is disabled in the form settings, although Google may process information under its own privacy policy. A local draft is not copied into the form. If you use Share, you choose the recipient in your device's share menu.

Purpose, legal basis and recipients. We use reports only to investigate and correct app content and to handle privacy enquiries. If you voluntarily include personal data in a report, our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in maintaining accurate content (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We do not request special-category data; if received inadvertently, we restrict processing and delete it when it is no longer needed to handle the report. The form owner can access responses, which are held in Google Forms/Drive. We do not sell reports or share them with advertisers. Government and other external links open outside the app, where the destination's privacy policy applies. Google may process data outside the EEA; it describes its transfer frameworks at https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks.

Retention and security. Your draft remains on the device until you delete it in the app or clear the app's data. In older versions, Android system backup may have included app data; previously created backups are governed by your device and backup-service settings. Google Forms reports are reviewed and deleted when no longer needed, no later than 12 months after receipt, unless longer retention is necessary for a legal claim or legal obligation. The form uses HTTPS and response access is limited to the owner's account. We cannot control the security of messages you choose to send through other apps.

Your rights. You may request access, rectification, erasure or restriction of your personal data and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Contact hello@callandcraft.com with enough detail to locate your report, but do not send a PESEL or ID scan. You can delete a local draft in the app and remove a saved PDF from your chosen folder. You may complain to Poland's data protection authority, UODO: https://uodo.gov.pl/.

Current scope. This version has no user accounts, analytics, advertising or active in-app purchases. We will update this policy and the applicable consent controls before adding them. Changes will be dated above.
